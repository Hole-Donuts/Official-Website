MAGICION Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what data the MAGICION mobile game (Android and iOS) collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. MAGICION is made by Holy Doughnut Studio ("we", "us").

The short version
  • You don't need an account to play. We never ask for your name, email or phone number in the game.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase to count how the game is played and to report crashes.
  • These services use your device's advertising ID and similar identifiers. You can limit or reset that ID at any time.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card or payment details.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see Deleting your data).

1. Data we collect

MAGICION collects data automatically through the services below. We don't sell your data.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

MAGICION shows ads served by Google AdMob. To choose and measure ads, AdMob collects the advertising ID, IP address, and device and app information, and may show ads based on your interests.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the first time you open the game you'll see a consent message from Google. You can choose personalised ads, non-personalised ads, or manage each partner. You can change your choice later from the game's settings menu.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and you only see non-personalised ads.

Read more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Google Firebase

We use these Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics: anonymous statistics on how the game is played. We see totals and trends, not who you are.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports so we can fix bugs.

Firebase processes data on Google servers, which may be outside your country. Read more: Privacy and security in Firebase.

4. The advertising ID

The advertising ID is a resettable ID from your phone's operating system. It does not contain your name, phone number or email. You can control it at any time:

Android

Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some phones: Settings → Google → Ads). Choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. After deleting it, apps receive only zeros instead of an ID.

iPhone and iPad

Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Turn off tracking for MAGICION, or turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track for all apps.

Turning these off doesn't remove ads, but the ads you see will no longer be based on your interests.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. They process your payment under their own privacy policies. We never receive your card number, billing address or payment account.

We receive only the information needed to deliver your purchase: which item you bought, when, and the order or transaction ID. For refunds, contact Google Play or Apple; for missing items, contact us with your order ID (see Contact).

6. Children

MAGICION is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

7. How long we keep data

  • Analytics data: up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: about 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you, then deleted.
  • Ad data is kept by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete your data in these ways:

  1. Data on your device: uninstall MAGICION, or clear its storage (Android: Settings → Apps → MAGICION → Storage → Clear storage). This removes your saved progress and settings for good.
  2. Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID as shown in section 4. New ad data can no longer be linked to the old ID.
  3. Analytics and crash data held by us: email holydoughnutofficial@gmail.com with the subject "MAGICION data deletion". Tell us your device type and, if you have it, the approximate date you installed the game. We will delete the analytics and crash data linked to your copy of the game within 30 days and confirm by email.

You don't need an account to request deletion, and deleting your data is free.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the CCPA in California, or Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent. Email us to use any of these rights. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For big changes we'll also tell you in the game.
