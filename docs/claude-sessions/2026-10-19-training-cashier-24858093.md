I built the cashier training game as **Meja Kasir** (SOP-KSR-01). It matches Pos Jaga Lobi and Clear Desk: one HTML file, the same dark look and fonts, an ID/EN switch, and the studio icons and license. It's open in the browser pane now.

How it covers each of your points:

- **Straight into gameplay:** there's no main menu. The first customer is already at the counter when the page opens. The SOP card on the desk and the **SOP Kasir** button are optional references.
- **First-person view of the desk:** the customer stands behind the counter. The cashier's desk holds the shopping basket, scanner and scale, POS screen, QRIS stand, card reader, UV lamp, cash tray, cash drawer and receipt printer.
- **Shows how a checkout works:** every customer goes through the same steps. The player greets the customer and checks the bottom of the basket. Then they scan each item, typing the code if a barcode is damaged and weighing produce by its PLU number. Cigarettes need an ID check for age 21+. Then comes TOTAL and payment:
  - **Cash:** check Rp50.000/Rp100.000 notes by backlight or UV, enter the amount received, count out the change, close the drawer.
  - **QRIS:** confirm only from the payment record on the POS, not the customer's phone.
  - **Debit:** the customer types their own PIN, and the card goes back to them.

  After payment come the receipt and a thank-you.
- **Win:** serve all 5 customers by the procedure. The shift report then lists what was handled for each customer.
- **Lose:** one procedure violation ends the shift. A window shows what went wrong, why it matters and the correct step. There are 21 different violations.
- **Different every replay:** each shift creates 5 new customers with different items and payment methods. Customers 2–5 each get one situation from a pool of 12: a counterfeit note, a fake QRIS proof, an underage buyer or one with no ID, an item hidden at the bottom of the basket, a friend asking you to skip an item, a customer asking you to type their PIN, and others. Some shifts also bring a visitor who isn't buying anything: either a fake "manager" asking for drawer cash, or someone asking to break a large note.

**Test results:**
- An automated player following the SOP passed every one of 12 shifts, which covered all 12 situations and both visitor types.
- I tested 9 kinds of mistakes, and each one ended the shift with the right explanation.
- I checked the desktop and phone layouts. On phones the scene is small, so the customer's lines are also shown in the bottom panel.
